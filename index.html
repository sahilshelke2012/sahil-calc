About Sahil Calc

Sahil Calc is a free online calculator designed for quick and easy calculations. You can add, subtract, multiply, divide and calculate percentages directly in your browser.

How to Use This Calculator

Enter numbers using the calculator buttons or your keyboard. Choose an operation and press equals to calculate the result. Use AC to start again or DEL to correct your input.

Features

Sahil Calc supports basic arithmetic, decimal calculations, percentages, keyboard input, calculation history, dark mode and light mode. It works on desktop and mobile browsers.
