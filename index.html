    About Sahil Calc

    Sahil Calc is a free online calculator designed for students and everyday users. It provides basic arithmetic, percentage calculations, marks percentages and averages in one convenient website. No account is required.

    Free Online Percentage Calculator

    To find a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 500 equals 100. Use our percentage calculator for quick results.

    Student Marks Percentage Calculator

    Calculate your school or exam percentage by dividing the marks obtained by the maximum marks and multiplying by 100. For example, 450 marks out of 500 gives 90%.

    Free Online Average Calculator

    Find the arithmetic mean of multiple numbers by adding all values and dividing by the number of values. For example, the average of 10, 20 and 30 is 20.
